Join the lab

We are looking for excited, motivated and curious new lab members — graduate students, visiting students, clinical fellows and post-docs — to join our team. Graduate students across Bay Area programs (UCSF Tetrad, UCSF BMS, UCSF–Berkeley Bioengineering, and others) are encouraged to apply.

How to apply

  1. Post-docs: send a CV, a short description of past and current research interests, key publications, and 3 reference contacts.
  2. Graduate students: apply through your Bay Area program and reach out about rotations.
  3. Everyone else: email us describing your research interests.
